Privacy policy

Last updated September 30, 2026.

creator-yt uses the YouTube Data API to manage channels that belong to its owner. When the owner signs in with Google, the tool gets permission to read and edit that channel's videos. The only data it touches is the channel's own video list, titles, descriptions, schedule times and privacy settings.

What is stored

An access token for each connected channel, kept in a file on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's. Nothing is sold, shared or used for ads.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions. Deleting the local token files removes everything the tool kept.

YouTube's terms apply: YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
